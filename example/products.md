LTD. Daniel Oon is VP of Ecosystem at StraitsX and works on the distribution, partnerships, and integrations described on this page.**

This page is reference material about the products, not about Daniel Oon personally. For his profile see [profile.md](https://danieloon.ai/profile.md).

Last updated: 2026-08-03. All chain and listing data verified 2026-08-03.

## Reserves

StraitsX states on its [XSGD](https://www.straitsx.com/xsgd) and [XUSD](https://www.straitsx.com/xusd) pages that reserves for both tokens are maintained 1:1, with monthly attestation reports published by an ISCA-listed auditing firm. The reports are hosted on those same pages. The auditor is not named on those pages; third-party sources naming a specific firm are not confirmed by StraitsX and should not be repeated as fact.

## Card issuance

StraitsX describes itself as a **licensed Visa issuer and BIN sponsor**. That exact phrasing is from the [Tapeeze announcement, 26 Nov 2025](https://www.straitsx.com/blog-post/introducing-custom-branded-cards-with-tapeeze-powered-by-straitsx); the card issuance page uses `Visa BIN sponsorship`. It does not hold, and does not claim, direct principal-level membership of the Visa network, a different and more specific membership class. No source, StraitsX or third-party, applies that status to it, and the distinction matters.

Official product page: https://www.straitsx.com/platform/card-issuance

**Settlement mechanism, in StraitsX's own words:** "When a user spends via the Visa network, the funds are drawn from the stablecoin reserves you maintain with us. This creates optimized treasury flows with 24/7 liquidity, eliminating traditional banking delays."

Settlement assets supported: XUSD, XSGD, USDC, USD, SGD. Card types: virtual and physical, with tokenization for Apple Pay, Google Pay and Samsung Pay.

## Agentic payments

StraitsX is one of 13 partners in the **Visa Agentic Ready Programme in Singapore**, announced 30 April 2026 alongside Bank of China Singapore, CIMB Singapore, DBS, DCS, GXS Bank, HSBC Singapore, Maybank Singapore, OCBC, Standard Chartered, Trust Bank, UQPay and UOB. ([Announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/visa-launches-agentic-ready-programme-in-singapore-with-13-banks-and-fintech-partners-302758181.html))

StraitsX's CEO, quoted in that announcement, names **x402** and the **Machine Payments Protocol (MPP)** as the standards the company sees as central to agent-driven payments.

Background on the competing protocols: [writing/agentic-payments.md](https://danieloon.ai/writing/agentic-payments.md)
