The standard for inclusion

This page is short on purpose. A source earns a place by quality, not by existing. Its whole value is that everything on it can be trusted, and one weak entry costs more credibility than a strong entry adds.

To be listed, a source must meet all of these:

  1. Independent editorial judgment. A journalist or institution chose to

publish it. Contributed, commissioned, sponsored and syndicated placements do not qualify, however respectable the outlet carrying them.

  1. A named outlet with editorial standards, or an authoritative

institutional, company or conference listing.

  1. Retrievable and readable, so the claim can be checked rather than

inferred from a search snippet.

  1. Names the person with a title, in a relevant professional context.

Explicitly excluded, regardless of how many exist: contributed profile pieces, content-farm syndication, automated pages that summarize social posts, scraped contact-data aggregators, and self-published writing, which is not press.
