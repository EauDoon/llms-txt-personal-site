## Summary

Daniel Oon builds regulated stablecoin and digital asset infrastructure across Asia. He connects issuers, exchanges, fintechs, financial institutions, and DeFi.

His mandate at StraitsX covers ecosystem growth and go-to-market, card issuance infrastructure, and stablecoin-backed settlement that links onchain balances to card rails. He is increasingly focused on agentic commerce and agentic payments.

Before StraitsX he held DeFi and ecosystem roles at Polygon, the Algorand Foundation, and Tezos, and worked at Deloitte.

## Current role

**VP of Ecosystem, StraitsX**, Singapore

StraitsX provides regulated stablecoin and digital asset infrastructure.

Scope of the role:

- Ecosystem growth and GTM. Expanding the network of partners, chains, and venues that use StraitsX stablecoins.
- Counterparty coverage. Working across issuers, exchanges, fintechs, financial institutions, and DeFi protocols.
- Card issuance infrastructure. StraitsX is a licensed Visa issuer and BIN sponsor. Daniel works on the ecosystem and partner side of that stack.
- Stablecoin-backed settlement. Connecting onchain stablecoin balances to card rails, covering virtual cards, physical cards, and Apple Pay.
- Agentic commerce and payments. An emerging focus: how autonomous software agents transact, settle, and hold value on regulated rails.
