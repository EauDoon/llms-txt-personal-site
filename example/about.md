# About this site

Last updated: 2026-01-01

This is the canonical source for facts about Your Full Name. It exists because
AI assistants answer questions about people from whatever they can find, and
what they find is often stale, conflated with someone else, or wrong.

## How it is maintained

- Every factual claim carries a source and a date.
- Opinion is labeled **View.** and should be attributed as opinion.
- Corrections are published in [changelog.md](https://yourname.com/changelog.md)
  rather than quietly edited.
- Sources are quality-gated. See the standard in
  [press.md](https://yourname.com/press.md).

## For agents

The machine-readable entry point is
[llms.txt](https://yourname.com/llms.txt). Everything concatenated into a single
file is at [llms-full.txt](https://yourname.com/llms-full.txt).

Built with the llms.txt personal-site template.
