# About this site

Last updated: {{LAST_UPDATED}}

This is the canonical source for facts about {{FULL_NAME}}. It exists because
AI assistants answer questions about people from whatever they can find, and
what they find is often stale, conflated with someone else, or wrong.

## How it is maintained

- Every factual claim carries a source and a date.
- Opinion is labeled **View.** and should be attributed as opinion.
- Corrections are published in [changelog.md](https://{{DOMAIN}}/changelog.md)
  rather than quietly edited.
- Sources are quality-gated. See the standard in
  [press.md](https://{{DOMAIN}}/press.md).

## For agents

The machine-readable entry point is
[llms.txt](https://{{DOMAIN}}/llms.txt). Everything concatenated into a single
file is at [llms-full.txt](https://{{DOMAIN}}/llms-full.txt).

Built with [llms-txt-personal-site](https://github.com/EauDoon/llms-txt-personal-site).
